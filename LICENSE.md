## Third-party Advanced input data

### AEMO demand

The Advanced bundle includes unaltered AEMO operational-demand source archives
for Australian NEM FY2026 (1 July 2025-30 June 2026).
Source: Australian Energy Market Operator (AEMO),
[NEMWeb market data](https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/electricity/national-electricity-market-nem/data-nem/market-data-nemweb),
`ACTUAL_OPERATIONAL_DEMAND_HH`.
AEMO's [Copyright Permissions Notice](https://www.aemo.com.au/privacy-and-legal-notices/copyright-permissions)
permits use of public AEMO Material with accurate and appropriate attribution.
Its [Privacy and Legal Notices](https://www.aemo.com.au/privacy-and-legal-notices)
also apply. NEMSweep modelling and results are independent of AEMO.

### Climate.OneBuilding weather

**All seven EPW files remain included in the Advanced ZIP, unaltered.** Original
filenames, all eight header lines and all weather records are preserved
byte-for-byte as downloaded from
[Climate.OneBuilding.org](https://climate.onebuilding.org/).
They are TMYx 2011-2025 typical-year profiles, not weather observations for FY2026.

Official citation:

> Lawrie, Linda K, Drury B Crawley. 2026. Development of Global Typical Meteorological Years (TMYx). https://climate.onebuilding.org

Copyright (c) 2014-2026 Climate.OneBuilding. All Rights Reserved.
